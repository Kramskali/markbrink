Security research

Architect of GhostHunter-NG, a temporal-forensics vulnerability scanner that mines historical web archives to surface forgotten assets, leaked credentials, and reintroduced exposures that live scanners miss.

Universal Structural Amnesia (July 2026) applies that engine across 29 targets in 15 sectors over 27 years (1998-2025), documenting 134 regression events where organizations reintroduced previously mitigated credential exposures. Quantitative findings remain subject to corpus release, substantive testing, and independent validation. Preprint available on request.

Guardrail-evaluation research includes a submission to 0din and a probe lab for testing AI safety claims against the systems that make them. Vulnerability scanning and AI safety research are separate programs, run to the same standard: verify with real runs before you claim anything.

Human-AI coupling research

Open inventions

Waste-to-Phosphor Road Marking Process (v1.0, February 2026) - released CC0 public domain. An open technical concept proposing conversion of spent PCB etchant and steel pickle liquor into copper-doped zinc sulfide phosphor that charges by day and glows after dark. Proposed economics: bench equipment about $250, production about $300 per ton against $2,000-$5,000 per ton commercial. Concept report, not a demonstrated production system - independent laboratory, environmental, safety, durability, and economic validation remains necessary before production or deployment.
