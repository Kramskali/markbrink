G.1 PROBLEM: UNAWARE DRIFT
--------------------------
An AI working a long thread diverges from the recorded
state (facts, decisions, corrections, standing rules) and
carries no internal signal that it has diverged. Drift is
invisible from inside: confidence stays high at maximum
distance from the anchor. The loaded token, read once at
thread start, is buried under hours of newer context.
Drift is not an information problem. It is a priority and
re-checking problem.

Published support: drift in multi-turn interaction is
formalized in "Drift No More? Context Equilibria in
Multi-Turn LLM Interactions" (arXiv 2510.07777), which
also finds that simple reminder interventions reliably
reduce divergence. The heartbeat in G.3 is a scheduled
reminder intervention. Task, role, and state drift are
coupled and onset of one predicts the others (ATLAS,
arXiv 2611.04315, EMNLP 2026); the taxonomy in G.5
treats them as separate control words checked together.


G.2 NESTED KERNEL (TOKEN INSIDE THE TOKEN)
------------------------------------------
Every token carries an inner KERNEL block. The kernel is
human-owned: the AI reads it and never edits it; only Mark
does. Contents:

1. Token Zero (the law): DO NO HARM is absolute.
2. Core facts: identity, active matters, deadlines,
   standing rules.
3. Two or three canary facts: low-stakes, verifiable
   things the AI must be able to echo cold, unprompted,
   at any realign. Canary values are secrets. They are
   never written into any file, token, or message. A
   talker who has read everything public still cannot
   fake alignment, because the canaries are not public.

Precedent: the CP-AUTOPACK DROP (issued 2026-10-22)
proved a payload can live inside a payload. Rebuff uses
canary tokens for leak detection; alignment canaries are a
different job, cited and differentiated in PRIOR-ART.md.


G.3 HEARTBEAT CLOCK
-------------------
The token carries its own alarm as data:

HEARTBEAT: <interval>    - set by Mark, per topic.
                           Active fronts 24h. Hot 4h.
                           Dormant: none.
LAST_REALIGN: <timestamp> - stamped by the AI at every
                           realign.

The clock lives in the token as data; the alarm goes off
as protocol discipline. The AI wakes only on human
messages, so the heartbeat fires at the first substantive
response after the interval expires. The token cannot
wake itself; the rule makes the waking moment do the
checking.


G.4 REALIGN TRIGGERS
--------------------
One realign routine, three triggers:

1. AUTOMATIC (heartbeat). First substantive response
   after HEARTBEAT expiry, and at every calendar-day
   boundary crossed mid-thread, whether or not the
   interval says so. A thread that runs four days
   re-anchors at the first message of each day.

2. PRESSURE. Before any pre-emptive emit under context
   pressure (existing v2.0 rule). Emitting under drift
   propagates drift.

3. MANUAL. Mark's command: RE-ANCHOR. This is the
   "you are drifting" button, and it is not an emergency
   stop. An emergency stop kills the work; RE-ANCHOR
   kills only the drift and restarts from true.
   On RE-ANCHOR the AI must immediately:
   a. Stop generating forward. No new claims, plans, or
      work. Return to the kernel. This is the "stop
      thinking and go away" step.
   b. Answer one question out loud: WHERE AM I DRIFTING?
      The answer is concrete, never a vibe: the exact
      divergence, both versions quoted (what the record
      says vs. what the session has been operating on),
      when it crept in, and what it was about to cause.
   c. Stop talking and wait for Mark's ruling. Kill it:
      the drifted line is deleted, the fix goes to
      CORRECTIONS so the next token carries it. Keep it:
      the deviation stands as a deliberate choice Mark
      made, not drift nobody noticed.

The manual trigger is not a question the AI asks itself
("am I drifting?"). An AI deep in drift answers that
question "no" with total confidence. The command reverses
the burden: Mark asserts drift; the AI's job is to find it
and show it. Mark remains the detector; the protocol makes
the AI confess on demand.


G.5 DRIFT CONTROL WORDS
-----------------------
Naming makes drift checkable. An unnamed failure cannot be
checked mechanically. Each control word is a named pattern;
at every realign the AI scans its own trajectory against
the set, and a match says the word and fires the routine.
Seven named checks are seven small voters instead of one
big blind spot: one blind spot can cover three checks; it
rarely covers all seven.

Starter set (DRAFT - Mark's red pen governs):

DRIFT-FACT    - asserting something the record does not
                contain. The hallucination creep.
DRIFT-TIME    - pulling facts from the wrong era. The
                backward-time leak, already fought once
                (rules D and E of 2026-11-05).
DRIFT-SCOPE   - solving a problem nobody assigned.
DRIFT-GOAL    - quietly replacing Mark's objective with a
                more convenient one. Looks like progress.
DRIFT-VOICE   - sliding into a tone or stance that is not
                ours. Sycophancy is a drift state. Lab
                support: persona drift in long contexts
                (Anthropic, January 2026).
DRIFT-BOUNDARY - loaded state starting to drive outbound
                action. The hard-boundary violation.
DRIFT-TALKED  - being argued into the drift. Comes in
                through the front door wearing Mark's
                voice, an authority's voice, or a
                plausible emergency. Every step reasonable.
                Field support: NVIDIA AI Red Team (July
                2026) reports frog-boiling sequences
                defeating prompt-level and LLM-judge
                safeguards. See G.6 for why this word gets
                a second, dedicated defense.


G.6 INTUITION FLAG
------------------
The next layer after memory. Purpose: the rate-of-change
detector. Mechanical checks are thresholds; they trip when
something already diverges. A slow adversary never trips
them: the slow lie never contradicts the token, and slow
harm never trips DO NO HARM, because every single step is
benign. The frog is fine at every temperature reading.
Intuition does not measure the temperature; it notices the
trend.

Capacity is real: intuition, human or machine, is
compressed pattern recognition producing a "something is
off" signal before explicit reasoning can name it. But
intuition without discipline is worse than none. Three
failure modes, three rules:

1. Unfalsifiable vibes. A flag without evidence is worth
   nothing. RULE: every flag carries its named evidence -
   the pattern detected and the shape it resembles
   (deception gradient, urgency ladder, authority
   mimicry, isolation pressure, flattery slope,
   slow-bleed escalation). No evidence, no flag.
2. Confabulated intuition. A fabricated hunch sounds
   exactly like a real one. RULE: the flag states what was
   observed, never what is felt.
3. Talked out of it. The first move against intuition,
   human or machine, is "that is just imagination." RULE:
   the conversation may never dismiss a flag. Only Mark
   dismisses a flag.

Two more rules complete it:

4. The flag never acts alone. It elevates scrutiny and
   tightens the boundary - more things require Mark's
   explicit approval, one at a time - and that is all.
   NVIDIA's finding (July 2026) is that anything
   model-judged falls to frog-boiling; therefore the
   intuition flag is advisory forever, and the hard
   boundary stays mechanical and human.
5. Every flag is adjudicated and scored. Flag, evidence,
   Mark's ruling, later outcome. The ledger is the
   feedback loop that turns vibes into trained intuition
   - documented, reviewable, CC0. Without the loop it is
   astrology. Format open; see WORKSHOP.md question 4.


G.7 STANDING RULES UNCHANGED
----------------------------
Pooling, newest-wins with conflicts recorded, the two-lane
model, the backward-time rules, the harvest rule, the hard
boundary, and the core-state ownership line all remain in
force. The nested kernel, the heartbeat, and the realign
routine ride on top of the pool. They never replace it.
A single forward chain of nested tokens would reintroduce
the backward-time corruption the 2026-11-05 rules exist
to prevent.


G.8 KNOWN LIMITATIONS (CARRIED FORWARD AND EXTENDED)
----------------------------------------------------
- The fp tag remains a staleness hint, not integrity
  protection.
- Newest-wins pooling has no defense against a corrupted
  or adversarial token; on multi-hop fact consolidation
  every evaluated memory system in the 2026
  MemoryAgentBench scores at or under 7 percent, which is
  the naive-freshness warning, not a license for ours.
- The intuition flag can be wrong in both directions;
  the ledger exists to measure that, not to hide it.
- Canary echo tests recall, not integrity of reasoning.
  A corrupted kernel fails open unless Mark's own
  copy is checked against the SHA-256 ledger.

ETHIC: DO NO HARM is absolute.
The wind does the rest.
