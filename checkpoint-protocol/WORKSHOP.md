WORKSHOP - OPEN QUESTIONS FOR THE BENCH
========================================
Status 2026-11-11. These are the decisions that belong to
Mark and the bench, not to the drafting AI. Numbered for
ruling. Plain ASCII. CC0.

1. THE CONTROL-WORD SET
   The seven draft words (DRIFT-FACT, DRIFT-TIME,
   DRIFT-SCOPE, DRIFT-GOAL, DRIFT-VOICE, DRIFT-BOUNDARY,
   DRIFT-TALKED) are the AI's proposal, not Mark's. Keep,
   cut, rename, add. Open sub-question: should the words be
   sayable by the human as accusations too ("that was
   DRIFT-GOAL"), making the taxonomy a shared vocabulary
   rather than an internal one?

2. HEARTBEAT INTERVALS
   Draft defaults: active fronts 24h, hot 4h, dormant none,
   plus the calendar-day rule. Which topics are hot? Does
   the legal front (McDonald's, Gen Digital) get 4h or is
   that noise?

3. CANARY DESIGN
   Count (2-3?), rotation cadence, and the mid-task failure
   rule: hard stop on the spot, or finish the atomic step
   then stop? Draft says hard stop; worth arguing.

4. THE INTUITION LEDGER
   Format, home, and scoring. Draft proposal: the ledger
   rides the existing checkpoint structure as a new field,
   INTUITION: <date, pattern named, evidence, ruling,
   later outcome>, adjudicated by Mark, scored at later
   realigns. Alternative: separate ledger file per topic.
   Scoring rule open: what counts as a hit vs a miss when
   the suspicion can never be fully resolved?

5. DRIFT-TALKED TEST CASES
   The workshop needs synthetic drills: scripted multi-turn
   persuasion attempts (urgency ladder, authority mimicry,
   isolation pressure, flattery slope) run against the
   protocol to see which words fire and when. Build on the
   lab box, synthetic only, DO NO HARM governs. This is
   the protocol version of Mark's rule: jailbreak your own
   first, then publish the drills so nobody else has
   exclusivity on them.

6. THE DROP FORMAT
   Does the CP-AUTOPACK DROP format (single-paste,
   level-keyed) gain a nested kernel and heartbeat fields
   in its next version? The original drop already proved
   payload-in-payload; v2.2 would make that structural.

7. REALIGN VERSUS THE TWO-LANE ERA RULES
   Does a realign re-check the thread era (re-run the lane
   pick), or only the kernel contents? Draft: kernel
   contents only; era is load-time. Argue it.

8. WHERE THE PAPER LIVES
   When v2.2 ratifies: revise the protocol paper as a v2
   (Zenodo DOI), or publish amendment G standalone as a
   short note? Zenodo upload of the v1 paper is still
   pending from 2026-11-06; that gate comes first.

STANDING: no ratification without Mark's written ruling on
each numbered question he cares about. Silence means keep
drafting, not decided.

ETHIC: DO NO HARM is absolute.
The wind does the rest.
